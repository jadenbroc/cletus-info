Cletus

A personal voice assistant

Cletus is a private, single-user assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public product or service. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is no way to sign up.

What it does

  • Answers questions by voice or text.
  • Reads its owner's Google Calendar and Gmail to summarize the day and new email.
  • With the owner's explicit approval each time, adds calendar events and saves email drafts. It never sends email.
  • Gives a morning brief with the weather, the day's schedule and important email.
