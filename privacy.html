Privacy policy

Cletus personal assistant · Effective November 5, 2026

Cletus is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It has no other users. This policy explains what data it handles and how.

Data Cletus accesses

  • Google Calendar: reads events and, only after the owner approves each one, creates new events.
  • Gmail: reads messages and, only after the owner approves each one, saves drafts. Cletus does not send, delete or modify email.
  • Yahoo Mail: reads messages without marking them as read.
  • Voice: speech is recorded only while the owner holds push-to-talk. It is converted to text on the owner's computer and is not stored or uploaded.
  • Location: the town the owner sets, used only to look up the weather.

How data is used

Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests: summarizing the schedule and email, answering questions, and preparing events or drafts for approval. It is not used for advertising or profiling, or for any other purpose.

Where data goes

  • Google sign-in tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • To answer a request, the relevant text (for example, an email or calendar event) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which generates the reply. Anthropic handles this data under its own commercial terms and privacy policy.
  • The owner's town is sent to Open-Meteo to look up the weather.
  • Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

Google user data

Cletus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Cletus keeps the current conversation in memory only while it is running, and does not keep a copy of email or calendar data. The owner can remove Cletus's access at any time from their Google Account under Third-party apps & services, and from Yahoo Account Security.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub page.
